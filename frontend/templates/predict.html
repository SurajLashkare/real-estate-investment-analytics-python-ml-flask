What is Rental Yield?
Rental Yield shows how much annual rent you earn compared to property price.
  • 🔻 Low: 2 – 3%
  • 🟡 Average: 3 – 4%
  • 🟢 Good: 4 – 6%
Example: ₹3L rent on ₹1Cr property → 3% yield
What is Crime Index?
Indicates safety level of the area.
  • 🟢 0.1 – 0.3 → Very Safe
  • 🟡 0.4 – 0.6 → Moderate
  • 🔴 0.7 – 1.0 → High Crime
What is Flood Risk?
Measures chances of flooding or water-logging.
  • 🟢 0.0 – 0.2 → No Risk
  • 🟡 0.3 – 0.5 → Occasional
  • 🔴 0.6 – 1.0 → High Risk Area
